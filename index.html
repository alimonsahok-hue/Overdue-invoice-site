Calculates overdue days and assigns a severity label.

STEP 4

Convert to File

Overdue status rules

Warning

1–14 days overdue. A friendly reminder may be enough.

Critical

15–30 days overdue. Direct follow-up is recommended.

Severe

More than 30 days overdue. Consider escalation.
